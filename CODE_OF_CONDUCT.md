# MForce Community Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We expect respectful, technically constructive participation. Treat other
contributors with dignity regardless of experience, identity or background.
Welcome good-faith questions, credit other people's work, and criticize code
or ideas rather than individuals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized
harassment, doxxing, publication of private messages or personal information
without consent, deliberate disruption, and misuse of security tools against
unauthorized targets.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
project-linked community spaces and interactions representing MForce.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, open a **non-sensitive** issue requesting a
private maintainer contact method. Do not post personal evidence publicly.
For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
The project currently does not publish a dedicated conduct-reporting inbox;
do not assume a confidential issue is available.

## Response

Maintainers may ask participants to edit comments, pause discussions,
remove abusive content or restrict participation as permitted by the
hosting platform. Reports will be considered based on available evidence
and severity. No fixed response deadline is promised.
